> ### About this activity
**Objectives:**
- Start creating a Data Management Plan in SciLifeLab Data Stewardship Wizard for your own project
- To identify gaps in your data management strategy
- To simplify communication around your data management strategy with others
> 
{: .callout}


## Create a Data Management Plan in SciLifeLab Data Stewardship Wizard

1. Go to [DS wizard](https://dsw.scilifelab.se/) and log in with your university credentials by clicking on the Life Science RI (university) button.
2. Create a project and name it according to your research-project
3. Select the template 'Vetenskapsrådet DMP Template'
4. Work yourself through the template and fill in the information that is relevant for your project
5. Invite collaborators to share the DMP with other members of your project
6. Try exporting the DMP in one or more formats

More guidance can be found in the Data Management Plan module, in the [DSW guide](https://dsw-appendix.scilifelab.se/dmp-guide/) and on [SciLifeLab RDM Guidelines](https://data-guidelines.scilifelab.se/topics/data-management-plan/)
